☀️ Solar Energy

Solar energy captures sunlight using photovoltaic (PV) panels and converts it into electricity.

Advantages

  • Zero emissions during operation
  • Low maintenance costs
  • Reduces electricity bills
  • Abundant and renewable source

🌬️ Wind Energy

Wind turbines convert kinetic energy from moving air into electrical power.

Benefits

  • Clean and renewable
  • Cost-effective at large scale
  • Creates green jobs
  • Reduces fossil fuel dependency
